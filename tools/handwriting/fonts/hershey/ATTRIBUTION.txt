Hershey fonts: futural.jhf, scripts.jhf, cursive.jhf; and, for corpus v2's maths, mathlow.jhf and
greeks.jhf (symbol sets: they only lend characters a writer's face lacks)
Source: https://github.com/kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts (downloaded 2026-10-14). COPYING beside this
file is that repository's own licence file, unmodified. The repository's hershey.txt, the file that
accompanied the original Usenet distribution, states the same usage conditions (re-read 2026-10-14).

COPYING has two parts. The GNU GPL v2 in its first half covers the repository's C library and
tools, which are NOT used here. The .jhf font data is covered only by the Hershey usage
conditions in its second half, quoted below.

    This distribution of the Hershey Fonts may be used by anyone for any purpose, commercial
    or otherwise, providing that:
      1. The following acknowledgements must be distributed with the font data:
         - The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the
           U. S. National Bureau of Standards.
         - The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by
           James Hurt
           Cognition, Inc.
           900 Technology Park Drive
           Billerica, MA 01821
           (mit-eddie!ci-dandelion!hurt)
      2. The font data in this distribution may be converted into any other format *EXCEPT*
         the format distributed by the U.S. NTIS (which organization holds the rights to the
         distribution and use of the font data in that particular format).

How Inkstone complies: the two acknowledgements travel with the data (here, in the corpus
manifest synth.py writes, and in the .ihwm header export.py writes), and the data is only ever
converted to synthetic pen trajectories -- never to the NTIS format.
